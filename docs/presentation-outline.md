# EthioVuln Final Presentation

## Slide 1: Title

EthioVuln - Web Vulnerability Assessment Platform

## Slide 2: Problem

Small organizations need affordable, understandable, and repeatable web
security testing.

## Slide 3: Product and Users

Show the dashboard and identify developers, SMEs, consultants, and security teams
as target users.

## Slide 4: Architecture

Present the frontend, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Redis/Celery, fuzzer, Nuclei, and ZAP
flow.

## Slide 5: Security by Design

Demonstrate authorization acceptance, DNS/private-IP blocking, rate limiting,
ownership checks, and safe findings handling.

## Slide 6: Detection Demo

Use an authorized lab target to demonstrate SQLi, reflected XSS, CSRF form
weakness, and missing security headers.

## Slide 7: Live Workflow

Create a scan, show progress and WebSocket logs, inspect evidence, and open the
finding remediation view.

## Slide 8: Reporting

Download and show the PDF executive summary, severity counts, CVSS, CWE, URLs,
and evidence.

## Slide 9: Validation

Show backend tests, frontend lint/build, migration checks, and a short results
table from the lab target.

## Slide 10: Business Model and Roadmap

Explain community, professional, consultant, and service offerings, followed by
scheduled scans, team workspaces, and remediation tracking.

## Slide 11: Lessons Learned

Discuss asynchronous orchestration, SSRF safety, detection confidence, and
building usable security reports.

## Slide 12: Conclusion

Restate the product value and show the repository/demo contact information.
